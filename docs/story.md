# WawaPacha story

The site answers six questions in one reading path. Registry links identify the
entries that support each view.

## What is the central-Pacific signal?

The central signal uses
[enso-is-ocean-atmosphere-pattern](../evidence/claims.toml),
[oni-definition-and-threshold](../evidence/claims.toml),
[nino-region-definitions](../evidence/claims.toml),
[weekly-nino-anomalies](../evidence/claims.toml) and
[roni-is-current-official-index](../evidence/claims.toml). The homepage starts
with the latest ONI record, the explanatory story, a sea-temperature view and
the weekly regional values.

## What is happening at the coast?

The coastal reading uses
[global-and-coastal-can-differ](../evidence/claims.toml),
[icen-definition](../evidence/claims.toml) and
[coastal-event-criterion](../evidence/claims.toml). After the sea-temperature
view, the homepage shows the coastal explanation and weekly regional values. The
historical route places the coastal index after the sea chronology.

## What is the official state in Peru?

The official-state reading uses [enfen-alert-states](../evidence/claims.toml).
The homepage places the official state after the weekly regional values and
shows the communiqué details when the data contains them.

## What do local observations show?

The local views use
[senamhi-hydrometeorological-stations](../evidence/claims.toml),
[chirps-rainfall-estimates](../evidence/claims.toml) and
[era5-historical-reanalysis](../evidence/claims.toml). The homepage links to
`/territorio` and `/historico` after the official-state section. The historical
route ends with station observations.

## What should the reader expect next?

The intensity outlook uses
[noaa-enso-strength-probabilities](../evidence/claims.toml). It follows the
local links on the homepage. The river view uses
[glofas-river-discharge-forecast](../evidence/claims.toml) and
[glofas-ensemble-percentiles](../evidence/claims.toml); the site keeps that view
separate from the ocean signal.

## What happened in past events?

The historical reading uses
[historical-coastal-chronology](../evidence/claims.toml). The homepage reaches
past-event exploration after the outlook. `/historico` shows the sea chronology
first, the coastal index second and station observations third.

## Reading path

The homepage presents the central signal, sea view, coast, weekly regional
values, official state, local links, intensity outlook and historical
exploration in that order. The historical route presents the sea chronology,
coastal index and station observations in that order.
